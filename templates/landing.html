Message studio
A room, as it happens: someone arrives, speaks, sends a voice note. Unread counts update in the rooms you're not looking at.

Made for small groups
who talk often.

What you get
01

Rooms are private.

Every room is invite-only. Share its link with the people you want in; nobody else can find it or read along.

02

Everything arrives live.

Messages, presence and unread counts travel over WebSockets, so a room updates the moment someone speaks. No refreshing.

03

Say it however it fits.

Type it, drop in a photo, or tap the mic and record a voice note. It all lives in the same conversation.

04

You can tell who's here.

Presence is part of the room. You see people arrive and leave, and who's around before you start talking.

How it works

Three steps
01

Create a room

Give it a name and, if you like, a line about what it's for.

02

Share the link

Each room has its own invite link. Send it wherever your people are.

03

Talk

They sign in, join with one click, and the conversation starts.
